Quick answers

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Profile → Delete account. All your data is erased within 30 days.

I bought something but didn't get it

Open the Shop and tap "Restore purchases". If it's still missing, write to us with the date of the purchase.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. What the purchase gave you (showcase, completed challenge, saved streak) is removed after the refund.

Why was my photo rejected?

Only photos taken live in the app count. Screenshots, photos of a screen, images from the web, or the same 67 photographed twice are rejected.
